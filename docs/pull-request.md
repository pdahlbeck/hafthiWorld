Adds the requested **coastal-food package** from #1 as three connected playable steps: **P fishing → L physical salt processing → H home delivery and meals**. The existing one worker, imported barter salt, household ration clock, seasons and winter-firewood economy all remain in the same persistent world.

- **Catch and store:** P orders a finite four-fish allowance. The worker walks to an original net-frame marker, completes an eight-second catch, picks up the actual fish, carries it back and unloads raw stock. Repeating P cannot replenish the catch.
- **Carry ingredients and salt:** L waits for two stored raw fish and one actually stored salt pouch. The worker carries salt and each fish separately to a new table, stands beside it for six seconds of work, then consumes the salt and transforms exactly those two fish. Each output is physically carried back to storage before another job begins. Offered/paid/in-transit salt is unavailable, and neither orders nor merely reaching the table grants output.
- **Supply and eat:** H also delivers stored salted fish into the owned pantry. Grain and fish share one 40-second ration clock, grain first when both exist; raw fish, processing output, shared stock, cargo and another home's food cannot be eaten. The HUD shows total food used and the fish subset, so grain consumption is total minus fish. A household can live on the four fish rations without planting this session.

All four fish and both salt pouches keep their entity IDs through their locations; consumed meshes hide. Salt now includes at-table/consumed states. The common one-cargo rule includes fish. Ready salting batches follow seed/home supply and precede new barter/production; new fishing follows the existing production queue. A processing batch finishes ingredient/work/output transport before a fresh task is accepted. Original procedural fish, net-frame and table assets are shared across both camera modes. No dependencies are added.
