A household could previously eat the only harvest, leaving no way to sow again. This adds an explicit choice: **V / Save seed** places a standing order to prepare one actually stored grain sack from each harvest into the next four-unit seed batch. The worker walks to the store and completes a four-second preparation task before new food deliveries or production work; current work finishes first. With H enabled, the remaining three sacks can become household meals. Without V, the existing four-meal finite cycle is preserved.

O can order another sowing after harvest once real seed is stored. The crop restarts at zero growth while all old food/cargo/store stocks remain intact. R rejects a new harvest until the crop is ripe and every previous sack has been consumed or saved, preventing the bounded four persistent sack slots from overwriting live inventory. The slots are reused only after their old contents are spent. Cumulative harvest, saved-seed and food-use counters persist across cycles and camera switches. Seed saving happens at most once per harvest, even if the next crop is already sown; repeated V/O/R cannot generate material.
