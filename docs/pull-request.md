The existing crop could be sown/grown continuously without a seasonal calendar. This adds the first small seasonal step from #1: a shared **year/season calendar**, spring/summer starts for new sowing jobs, winter crop-growth suspension and original seasonal meadow/grass palettes. A session starts in year 1 spring; each season lasts 120 simulation seconds and a year lasts 480 seconds.

O can queue a valid sow order in autumn/winter without charging seed. The worker can do other jobs and begins sowing when spring/summer opens under the existing priorities. Already started seed transport/sowing finishes across a boundary. Planted crops retain their exact progress through winter and resume in spring; ripe harvests, household rations and all other existing material flows continue. Long updates calculate actual nonwinter growth time across season/year boundaries instead of applying only the ending season's rate.

Calendar time advances once per world fixed tick, independently of cameras, worker count and the presentation sun. Tab/T/Y never reset or skip it. The HUD reports year, season, remaining seconds and the sowing window. Meadow/grass colors change by updating existing shared materials at season boundaries; no landscape, worker or cargo entities are replaced. Pines remain evergreen. No controls or dependencies are added.
