# Meadow albedo study provenance

`assets/materials/meadow-color.png` is an original image generated for this
project with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-10-22, then copied unchanged into the asset
pack. It is a 1254 × 1254 RGB image. It is not a photograph, a calibrated scan or
an extracted asset from the user-supplied village image. The normal/roughness
maps remain the procedural surface maps; their microstructure is illustrative.
The renderer blends this albedo into soil at the beach and worn yard paths.
The generated image is a tiling study; perfect edge matching is not guaranteed.
Runtime repeat sampling and mip chains support its non-power-of-two dimensions.

Generation prompt:

> Create one original professional videogame PBR BASE COLOR ALBEDO TEXTURE of a
> dry Baltic limestone meadow floor, about 2 metres by 2 metres of ground viewed
> EXACTLY straight down in orthographic projection. Seamlessly tileable on all
> four edges. Dense short fine natural grey-green and olive meadow grass
> intermixed with irregular worn patches of grey-beige chalky fine soil, tiny
> crushed white limestone granules, sparse dry straw and tiny clover leaves.
> Ground must feel organic, richly textured and convincing at walking distance
> but coherent and quiet from an elevated strategy camera. Realistic miniature
> botanical scale, natural irregular distribution, understated slightly
> desaturated colors. Flat diffuse neutral illumination ONLY: no shadows, no
> ambient occlusion, no specular highlights, no directional light, no perspective,
> no depth of field. This is a raw square texture for a new original 3D game, not
> a scene or a screenshot. No large rocks, no plants taller than the short turf,
> no flowers, no buildings, no people, no border, no labels, no text, no texture-map
> layout or swatches. Entire square is a single continuous perfectly overhead
> ground surface.
