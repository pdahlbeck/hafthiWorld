# Original coastal material studies, 2026-10-23

Both images were generated for this project with OpenAI ImageGen. The PNGs are
unaltered outputs, embedded in the executable. They are illustrative original
material studies, not scans or archaeological evidence. No third-party game art
or engine code was imported. Image dimensions: 1254 square for both reed and pine. `bake_reed_normal.py` generates a separate analytic micro-normal; it does
not reconstruct the image's geometry or edit the generated image.

## Reed albedo (`thatch-color.png`)

Create a single seamless tileable physically plausible albedo texture for an
original historical 3D game: a close orthographic perfectly flat scan of
weathered reed thatching, fine narrow grey beige dry reed stalks packed in
parallel vertically from top to bottom. Dense fine fibers, irregular split
stalks, darker crevices and subtle lichen and weathering, no regular corrugation,
no roof geometry, no perspective, no illumination, no shadows, no vignetting,
no borders, no text. Whole square is filled edge to edge with material, reed
direction vertical. Neutral muted grey straw, not yellow, no specular shine.
Very realistic detailed surface for a small Scandinavian medieval coastal house
roof.

## Scots pine branch (`pine-bough.png`, transparent)

Original realistic 3D game foliage texture cutout, single irregular Scots pine
bough viewed straight from above in orthographic projection, grey brown slender
forked twig starting at bottom center and growing toward upper center,
branching to left and right, bushy clusters of very fine long dark olive green
pine needles, naturally uneven silhouette with many gaps and holes, mature
Baltic coast Scots pine, not spruce not fern. Fill central 90% of square with
this one broad asymmetric fan shaped branch. Completely transparent background
around branch AND through spaces between needles. Flat neutral diffuse lighting
no cast shadow no vignette no text no border no ground no tree trunk. Botanical
believable needle clusters, muted green not teal, crisp fine detail. This will
be alpha-clipped on small branch cards in a real-time 3D forest.

These prompts express the desired image characteristics, not a guarantee of
perfect tile continuity, calibrated reflectance or botanically exact needles.
